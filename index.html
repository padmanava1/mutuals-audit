Account Safety & Rate Limits Guide

How to clean 1,000+ accounts with 0% risk of account blocks or bans.

⚠️ Why 1-Click "Bulk Unfollow" Bots Get Banned

Third-party apps that claim to "bulk unfollow 500 accounts in one click" require your login credentials or session tokens. Instagram's anti-abuse engine detects non-official API calls and foreign device signatures immediately, resulting in Action Blocked checkpoints, shadowbans, or permanent account suspensions.

Meta Platform Hourly & Daily Limits:

  • • Hourly Safe Velocity: Maximum 40–60 unfollows per hour.
  • • Daily Safe Cap: Maximum 120–180 unfollows per day (recommended: 50–100/day).
  • • Account Age Factor: Accounts less than 6 months old should stay under 30/hour and 80/day.

📱 Secret Native Mobile App Shortcut:

You can unfollow 50 accounts in ~45 seconds natively without loading profiles:

  1. Open the Instagram mobile app → go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Following → scroll to Categories at the top.
  3. Tap "Least Interacted With" (shows ~50 accounts you haven't engaged with in 90 days).
  4. Tap the Following button directly next to each name to unfollow with 1 tap.

🗓️ Recommended 7-Day Plan for 1,000+ Accounts:

Set Mutuals Audit's Safe Daily Pacing to 100 accounts/day. Do one session of 50 in the morning with Speed Queue, and one session of 50 in the evening. In 7 to 10 days, your follow list is completely clean with zero risk to your account.
